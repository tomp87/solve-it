We collect the bare minimum — a first name, not an email or real identity. The AI tutor stays focused on the maths question in front of it, and includes a safety check that flags any message suggesting a child might be distressed, so you can see and follow up.

What if my child doesn't get on with it?

Does it cover my child's exam board?

Questions are written in general GCSE style rather than copied from any board's actual papers, so they work across AQA, Edexcel, and OCR — the underlying maths doesn't change by board.

Can more than one of my kids use it?

Yes — one subscription, one family access code, and each child gets their own name and their own progress tracking under it.
