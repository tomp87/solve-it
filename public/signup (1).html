Questions parents actually ask

Is it safe for my child to use?

We collect the bare minimum — a first name, not an email or real identity. The AI tutor stays focused on the maths question in front of it, and includes a safety check that flags any message suggesting a child might be distressed, so you can see and follow up.

Will I be charged during the free trial?

No — Stripe needs a card on file to start the trial, but nothing is taken until day 7. Cancel any time before then and you won't pay anything at all.

What if my child doesn't get on with it?

Cancel any time — during the trial or afterwards. There's no minimum term and no awkward retention calls. If you cancel after being charged, you'll keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Does it cover my child's exam board?

Questions are written in general GCSE style rather than copied from any board's actual papers, so they work across AQA, Edexcel, and OCR — the underlying maths doesn't change by board.

Can more than one of my kids use it?

Yes — each child just needs their own subscription. At £4.99/month each, two kids practising is £9.98/month total, with each getting their own login and their own progress tracking.
