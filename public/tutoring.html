Of course, parents are always welcome to join or listen in.

What times are available?

Any time from 7am to 8pm UK time, including weekends.

What if we need to cancel a lesson?

Just let Tom know at least 24 hours before, and there's no charge.

Is the Solve It subscription really free?

Yes. While your child has regular lessons, their Solve It account is included at no extra cost.
