SOHCAHTOA, in one line

sin(θ) = opp/hyp  ·  cos(θ) = adj/hyp  ·  tan(θ) = opp/adj

In a right-angled triangle, once you know one angle (other than the right angle) and one side, you can find any other side using the correct ratio above — whichever one connects the side you know to the side you want.

The trick is identifying which sides are "opposite," "adjacent," and "hypotenuse" relative to the angle you're given — the hypotenuse is always the longest side, opposite the right angle, and the other two swap roles depending on which angle you're looking from.
