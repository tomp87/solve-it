Privacy Policy — Solve It

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what information Solve It ("we", "us") collects, why, and what rights you and your child have over it. We've tried to write this so a school-age reader could understand it too, not just a parent — that's a deliberate choice, not an accident.

Who we are

Solve It is provided by [YOUR NAME / BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS]. Data protection registration number: [ICO REGISTRATION NUMBER]. Contact: [YOUR EMAIL].

What information we collect

From the parent or adult who signs up and pays:

  • Name and email address
  • Payment details — we don't see or store these ourselves; they go directly to our payment processor, Stripe, who handle them securely on our behalf.

From the student using the app:

  • A first name (or chosen display name) — used only to identify their own progress, not verified as a real identity.
  • Which maths questions they've answered, whether they got them right, and their streak/progress per topic.
  • If they use the "Ask for help" tutor chat: the messages they type, and the AI's replies. These messages are sent to Anthropic (the company that provides the underlying AI model) in order to generate a response, along with the maths question being discussed — but not the student's name or any other identifying information.

We deliberately do not collect: the student's email address, date of birth, school name, home address, or any other information beyond what's needed to run the practice and tutoring features.

Why we collect it, and our legal basis

  • To provide the service you've subscribed to (legal basis: contract — fulfilling what we agreed to provide when you signed up).
  • To let a student see their own progress over time (same basis).
  • Where a user is under 13, we rely on parental consent rather than the child's own consent, in line with UK data protection law. If you are a parent and did not give this consent, please contact us immediately.

Who we share information with

  • Stripe (payment processing) — handles payment details; we never see full card numbers.
  • Supabase (database hosting) — stores account and progress data securely on our behalf.
  • Anthropic (AI provider) — receives tutor chat messages and the associated maths question in order to generate hint responses. Anthropic does not receive the student's name.
  • We do not sell personal data to anyone, for any reason. We do not use student data for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles of children.

Children's privacy — our specific commitments

Because this service is used by children, we commit to:

  • Never using nudge techniques, streaks, or notifications designed to pressure a child into spending more time in the app than they want to.
  • Keeping all default settings at the most private option — nothing is shared publicly, and there is no public profile or leaderboard visible to other users.
  • Not using a child's data to build an advertising profile, on this service or anywhere else.
  • Reviewing this policy and our practices regularly against the ICO's Children's Code (Age Appropriate Design Code).

Safety monitoring on the tutor chat

The tutor chat includes a basic automated check for messages that suggest a child might be distressed, unsafe, or disclosing harm. If a message matches, it's stored (along with the student's name and the message itself) so the parent or guardian on the account can see and follow up — this is visible only to the account holder, via the dashboard, using the same passcode used to manage the subscription.

This is a simple keyword-based check, not a clinical or professional safeguarding tool. It will miss some things and occasionally flag something harmless. It exists as an extra layer, not a replacement for paying attention to how your child is getting on.

If your child discloses something concerning in the tutor chat: our AI tutor is designed to stay focused on maths, and is not a substitute for a trusted adult, a teacher, or professional support. If you're ever worried about something your child has typed or been told in the app, please contact us at [YOUR EMAIL] and, if it's urgent, please also speak to a trusted adult, your child's school, or the relevant support service directly — don't wait for our response.

Your rights

You (or, on behalf of a child, their parent/guardian) can ask us to:

  • See what data we hold about you or your child
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete the account and all associated data
  • Export progress data in a readable format

To do any of these, email [YOUR EMAIL]. We'll respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the ICO, at ico.org.uk — though we'd really appreciate the chance to sort out any concern directly first.

Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect data — encrypted connections, hashed passwords/passcodes (we never store these in plain text), and access limited to what's needed to run the service. No system is perfectly secure, and if we ever discover a data breach affecting your information, we will tell you and the ICO without undue delay, as required by law.

Cookies and local storage

The app stores a login token in your browser's local storage so you don't have to log in every time. This is necessary for the app to function and isn't used for tracking or advertising. We don't use third-party advertising or analytics cookies.
